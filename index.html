About

M.Sc. Aerospace Engineering student at the Technical University of Munich, specialising in thermal-fluid systems, CFD, and model-based engineering. Currently working at BMW Group as a simulation engineer in R&D Power Systems.

My experience spans system-level thermal-electrical modelling in Modelica/Dymola, high-fidelity CFD with ANSYS and STAR-CCM+, and data-driven approaches including neural ODE system identification. I'm passionate about thermal engineering and propulsion, and applying advanced simulation methods to real aerospace problems.
